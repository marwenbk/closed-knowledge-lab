# Consultation and support hours

All hours use the **America/Sao_Paulo** time zone, presented to users as Brasília time.

## Consultations

| Service | Days | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| General practice | every day | 24 hours |
| Psychology | Monday through Saturday | 08:00–20:00 |
| Nutrition | Monday through Friday | 09:00–18:00 |
| Dermatology | Monday through Friday | 08:00–17:00 |

- **General practice:** available 24 hours every day.
- **Psychology:** Monday through Saturday, 08:00–20:00.
- **Nutrition:** Monday through Friday, 09:00–18:00.
- **Dermatology:** Monday through Friday, 08:00–17:00.

General practice operates continuously. The other specialties listed above have specific service windows and must not be described as available around the clock.

## Support

- **General and technical support:** every day, 07:00–23:00.
- **Billing support:** Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00.

## How to interpret hours

A plan including a specialty does not change that specialty's hours. To answer whether someone can use a service on a specific day or at a specific time, combine plan coverage with this availability table.

## Combination examples

A person may have a covered specialty and still need to wait for its next operating window. Dermatology coverage, for example, does not make dermatology continuously available. A supported answer combines the specialties document with the 08:00–17:00 window on the days listed above.

Psychology ends at 20:00 and includes Saturday. Nutrition ends at 18:00 and operates only on the defined weekdays. General practice is the only specialty in this table described as available all day, every day.

## Consultations and support use different schedules

Support hours do not control clinical-service hours. General practice may be available while billing support is closed. General support may be open while a scheduled specialty is unavailable.

Billing questions use the window from 09:00 to 17:00. General and technical questions use the wider window from 07:00 to 23:00.

## Safe interpretation

The assistant must not convert these hours to another time zone without an approved rule. It also must not infer hours for pediatrics, cardiology, or endocrinology from other specialties. If a question says only “after hours” or “on the weekend,” the assistant must identify the service before selecting a schedule.
